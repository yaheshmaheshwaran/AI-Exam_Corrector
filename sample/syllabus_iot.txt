CCS356          INTERNET OF THINGS AND ITS APPLICATIONS          L T P C
                                                                   2 0 2 3

COURSE OBJECTIVES:
 To understand the architecture of embedded systems and the Internet of Things.
 To learn the communication protocols used in IoT.

UNIT I          EMBEDDED SYSTEMS                                           9
Embedded system definition and characteristics – Processor in an embedded system – Microcontrollers
and microprocessors – Memory, timers and interrupts – Embedded software, firmware and device drivers –
Real-time operating systems: task scheduling, priority inversion, inter-task communication.

UNIT II         INTRODUCTION TO IoT                                        9
Definition and characteristics of IoT – Physical design of IoT; things and protocols – Logical design
of IoT; functional blocks, communication models and APIs – IoT enabling technologies; wireless sensor
networks, cloud computing, big data analytics – IoT levels and deployment templates.

UNIT III        IoT COMMUNICATION AND CONNECTIVITY                         9
IoT protocol stack – IEEE 802.15.4, ZigBee, Bluetooth Low Energy – Wi-Fi and LoRaWAN – Application
protocols: MQTT, CoAP, HTTP and REST – Publish/subscribe versus request/response – IPv6 and 6LoWPAN.

UNIT IV         PROTOTYPING WITH ARDUINO AND RASPBERRY PI                  9
Sensors and actuators – Arduino board, programming and interfacing – Raspberry Pi architecture and
Python programming – Interfacing sensors with Raspberry Pi – Sending data to the cloud.

UNIT V          IoT APPLICATIONS AND CASE STUDIES                          9
Smart home and home automation – Smart city: smart parking, smart lighting – Industrial IoT and
predictive maintenance – Smart agriculture – Health monitoring with wearables – IoT security and privacy.
                                                                  TOTAL: 45 PERIODS

COURSE OUTCOMES:
At the end of the course, the students will be able to:
CO1: Explain the architecture of embedded systems and real-time operating systems.
CO2: Describe the physical and logical design of IoT.
CO3: Compare IoT communication and application protocols.
CO4: Build IoT prototypes with Arduino and Raspberry Pi.
CO5: Design IoT solutions for real-world applications.

TEXT BOOKS:
1. Arshdeep Bahga and Vijay Madisetti, "Internet of Things – A Hands-on Approach", Universities Press, 2015.
2. Raj Kamal, "Embedded Systems: Architecture, Programming and Design", McGraw Hill, 2017.

REFERENCES:
1. Adrian McEwen and Hakim Cassimally, "Designing the Internet of Things", Wiley, 2014.
